# v1.0.8

## Fix: `jsonbMerge` casts a compound patch as a whole

`jsonbMerge(target, patch)` rendered `COALESCE(target, '{}'::jsonb) || patch::jsonb`, so a patch that is
itself an expression — `sql\`${a} || ${b}\`` — had the cast bound to its LAST operand only:
`… || a || b::jsonb`. With `a` and `b` jsonb already, the result happened to be the same; with text halves
of one JSON document, `jsonb || text` resolved to text concatenation and the statement failed (or, as a
read, produced text). The patch is now parenthesised before its cast:
`COALESCE(target, '{}'::jsonb) || (patch)::jsonb`. A plain value or a single call renders the same result.

- `src/query/conditions.ts` (`jsonbMerge`)
- `tests/mutations/insert-update-delete.test.ts` ("jsonbMerge casts a compound patch as a whole")
